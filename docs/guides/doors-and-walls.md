# Doors and walls

Walls stop sight and movement. A door is a wall that can be opened. This
guide is about working them during play; drawing them is the Wall tool's job.

## As the Game Master

Right-click a wall or a door on the board.

| You right-click | The menu offers                                                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A wall          | **Make this a door**                                                                            |
| A door          | **Open** or **Close**, **Lock** or **Unlock**, **Lock as a wall**, **Make it an ordinary wall** |
| A hidden door   | The same, and **Show it to the table**                                                          |

**Double right-click** a door to lock it as a wall straight away, without the
menu.

### The three ways a door can be shut to the players

- **Unlocked.** Anybody at the table can open and close it.
- **Locked.** The players see a door and cannot open it. You still can.
- **Locked as a wall.** The door is shut, locked and hidden. The players see
  no door there: it blocks their sight and their way like the wall around it.
  You see it in its own colour. **Show it to the table** makes it an ordinary
  locked door again; **Unlock** then lets them through.

Locking as a wall shuts an open door first.

## As a player

Right-click a door to open or close it. A locked door says **Locked**; ask
your Game Master.

On a touchscreen, hold a finger still on the door for the same menu. See
[Playing on a touchscreen](./playing-on-a-touchscreen.md).

## From the keyboard

The door menu opens from the pointer only. A Game Master can do everything
above from the Interaction tool: select the door, and its panel has the same
controls.
