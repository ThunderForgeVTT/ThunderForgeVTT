# Your first world

When you create a world, it comes with a **Starting Scene**. You can have
that scene open on one of our maps, so your first session has a board to
play on straight away.

## Picking the starting map

The create form shows **Starting map**: a card for each map the instance
offers, and **None**. The form picks the instance's default map for you.
Choose another card, or **None**, before you press **Create world**.

The map arrives as a map import would bring it: its background, its size
and grid, and its walls, doors and lights. Its edges are walled, so a token
cannot walk off the side of the map (see [Doors and walls](doors-and-walls.md)).

**None** gives you a blank Starting Scene, the same as a world made before
starting maps existed. You can put a map on it later with the map import
tool.

If the map cannot be put on the scene, your world is still created, on a
blank scene, and you are told so.

## Whose maps these are

The maps are by **MBRound18**, from
[vtt-maps](https://github.com/mbround18/vtt-maps), and you can browse them
in the [catalog](https://vtt-maps.dnd-apps.dev/catalog). They are offered
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and
the copies here are offered under the same licence.

That licence asks for the credit every time a map is shown, so ThunderForge
shows it:

- under the map cards on the create form;
- on the board, as a short line that opens into the full credit, with its
  links, when you point at it or move to it with the keyboard;
- in the scene list, and on the scene's own page.

## When there are no maps

An instance's administrator decides which maps are offered. If there are
none, the create form offers only **None**.
