# Inviting players to your world

A **world link** brings a player into your world. It is for people who
already have an account on this ThunderForge instance: a world link never
creates an account.

## Making a link

Only the world's owner and its Game Masters can make links. Open the world's
**Players** page and find **World links**.

1. Pick **Uses**. The default is **No limit**. You can instead allow from 1
   to 50 joins.
2. Pick **Expires after**: 1 day, 7 days (the default), 30 days, or
   **Never**.
3. Press **Generate Join Link**, then **Copy link**, and send it to your
   players however you like.

The world page's **Players** card shows how many links are working, and
takes you to the Players page.

## When a use counts

A use is spent only when someone actually joins: when their account becomes
a new member of the world. Nothing else spends one:

- opening the link, or looking at its join page;
- a chat app or a search engine fetching the link to preview it;
- refreshing the page, or pressing **Join** twice;
- someone who is already in the world, you included;
- a join that fails.

Each link in the list shows its joins, and, when it has a limit, how many
uses it has left. The list updates on its own when someone joins.

## Taking a link back

Press **Revoke permanently** on the link, and confirm. From then on the link
admits nobody. Anyone who already joined with it keeps their place.

A link that is revoked, expired or used up moves under **Past links**.

## What the person with the link sees

They sign in with their existing account, and join. If the link no longer
works, the join page tells them why: it was revoked, it expired, it is used
up, or it is not a link at all. It sends them back to you for a new one.

## Someone new to the instance

A world link cannot make an account, whether they try to register or to sign
in with another service. Ask the instance's administrator to send them an
**instance invitation** first. Once they have an account, your world link
works for them.
