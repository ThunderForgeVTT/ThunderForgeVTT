# Characters for your players

A player plays one character in a world. There are three ways they come by
it.

## You make it, they pick it

Open **Players** in your world. Under **A character to claim**, type a name
and press **Create character**. It is a player character on your world's game
system, and it is offered to the next player who joins: they see it on the
screen that follows your join link and press **Select**.

Make as many as you have seats. A character somebody has picked is no longer
offered to anybody else.

Open the character from the link that appears to fill in its sheet, or leave
that to the player: picking a character lets them edit it.

## You give it to them

On the same page, each player's card has a character picker. Choose a
character there to give it to that player, whether or not they have joined a
session yet.

## They make their own

On your world's page, under **Campaign Settings**, turn on **Allow players to
create their own actors**. A joining player is then offered a name box and a
look builder instead of waiting on you.

## Taking one back

Open the character's page and press **Un-claim**. It can be offered again
from there.

## Bringing one in from a sheet

A player who already has the character on D&D Beyond, or on a Roll for Shoes
sheet, can bring it in from its PDF. See
[Bringing a character in](./bringing-a-character-in.md).
