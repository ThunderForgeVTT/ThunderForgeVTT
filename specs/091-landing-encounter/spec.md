# Feature Specification: One Room on the Front Page, Played on the Real Engine

**Feature Branch**: `091-landing-encounter`
**Created**: 2026-10-13
**Status**: Draft (spec only; plan.md and tasks.md not yet written)
**Input**: The owner, 2026-10-13:

- Of the options surveyed for a more gamified thunderforge.dev, the owner
  chose a one-room encounter on a real map ("Option B"), with the engine
  dice throw built first. Spec 090 built that throw and the frame it runs
  in. This spec is the room.
- The goals are fixed: walk to the door (a wall refuses the move), open the
  door (light spills through), find the chest, and roll to open it (the DC
  shown first, the dice thrown on the engine).

## Why

The landing's map section says "Everything on the map is real." Today the
visitor can only look at that claim: the legend draws walls, doors and
lights over a picture. The hero's battle mat lets them bump a wall, but
that wall is a JavaScript copy of the rule, drawn in SVG.

The engine already does every part of the claim: walls that refuse a move,
doors that open, light that stops at walls and spills through a doorway,
sight, things on the map that respond, and dice that land on the server's
numbers. Spec 090 put the engine on the page behind a click. This spec
gives the visitor one small room to play on it, in under a minute, without
leaving the page and without a server.

The page plays the server's part, and says so. ThunderForge's principle is
"one authority", so the room must never pretend a server is there.

## What exists

Read on 2026-10-13 against branch `090-landing-engine-dice` at `146a3826`.

### The landing, after spec 090

  `<main>`: Dream, the dice section (`#dice`, lazy `DiceTray`, lines 26-41),

  `createHero(spec).token()`. The art is an SVG `data:` URL (lines 56-71).
  Its wall rule `crossesWall` (lines 102-108) counts a crossing only when the
  ends lie strictly on opposite sides. A move that touches a wall's end is
  not refused.
- **Map legend** (`src/sections/MapLegend.tsx`): the atlas opens on `demo`,
  The Proving Ground. It draws `/maps/demo.webp` (line 105), so the image is

- **e2e**: `apps/landing/e2e/` holds `a11y`, `dice-tray`, `engine-frame`,
  `hero`, `looks`, `map-legend` and `telemetry` specs. The `landing` slice
  (`scripts/e2e/slices.json`, lines 636-649) owns `apps/landing/**` and
  `packages/dice-throw/**`. It builds the demo first, so `/demo/` is served
  beside the landing.
- The e2e reads the engine by importing the same module URL the page
  imported (`e2e/engine-frame.spec.ts`, lines 41-57). Any `#[wasm_bindgen]`
  read is reachable this way.

### The engine frame (spec 090)

- `src/engine/EngineFrame.tsx`. Props (lines 22-32): `id`, `poster`,
  `action`, `setup(send)` and `onState(state, send)`.
  - `setup` is called once, after boot (line 121).
  - The frame renders its own `<canvas aria-hidden tabIndex={-1}>` (line
    215) and keeps it out of the tab order.
  - It pauses drawing out of view with `set_rendering` (line 162).
  - It holds back without WebGL2, with `saveData`, or with `deviceMemory`
    below 4 (`holdBack`, line 40).
  - The stage is a fixed 960×540 board (line 37), scaled to the frame with
    CSS `scale` (`engine-frame.css`, lines 19-31).
- `src/engine/loadEngine.ts` reads `/demo/engine.json`, streams the wasm,
  imports the JS and calls `mod.start(canvasSelector)` (line 112). Its
  `Engine` type (line 18) names only `start`, `apply_world_command`,
  `engine_stats` and `dice_landed`.
- **One engine per page.** `start` returns at once if it has already run
  (`startup.rs:43`, `ENGINE_STARTED`). It does not throw. A second
  `EngineFrame` on the page would load, "start", report `running`, and draw
  nothing, because the engine is already bound to the first frame's canvas.
  The JS module is cached by URL, so both frames would share one instance.
- **Pause.** `set_rendering` (`sdk.rs:349`, `plugins/render_pause.rs`)
  switches the loop to `UpdateMode::Reactive`. Every command wakes it for one
  update.
- **Telemetry.** A failure is dispatched as `thunderforge:engine-frame-failed`
  (`src/engine/frameEvents.ts`). `src/telemetry.ts` (line 44) counts it as
  `engine.load_failed`.

- `apps/landing/public/maps/demo.json` is generated by
  `scripts/prepare-static.mjs` (lines 18-49) from the demo's maps. It holds
  `name`, `width` 4095, `height` 2340, `gridSize` 117, 33 walls and 12
  lights. The walls carry no ids.
- In squares from the map's centre (demo.json units ÷ 117):
  - The outer wall runs x −17.5 to 17.5, y −10 to 10.
  - A **middle room** spans x −9.5 to 4.5 and y −2 to 10. No light stands
    in it.
  - Its wall at y = −2 holds a **closed door** at x −3.5 to −2.5.
  - Beyond that door lies a **long hall** (y −10 to −2), lit by four lights
    at y ≈ −6. One of them stands at x −2.56, five squares in radius.
  - A second closed door, at x −14.5 to −13.5, leads to the west room. A
    spiral of walls fills the east.
- `demo.webp` is **1.95MB** (1,953,402 bytes), 4095×2340. That is one pixel
  under a 4096 texture. WebGL2 only guarantees 2048.
- Credit: MBRound18, CC BY-SA 4.0, github.com/mbround18/vtt-maps. The legend
  already shows it.

### What the engine offers the room

**Commands**, through `apply_world_command(json)` (`sdk.rs:363`, parsed in
`parse_command`, lines 95-353). A malformed command is reported as an
`sdkError` event (line 381), never dropped.

- **Scene**: `set_scene_background` (path, width, height), `set_scene_grid`
  (size, map size, origin), `set_camera`, `fit_camera_to` and `focus_token`
  (with `immediate`).
- **Tokens**: `upsert_token` (id, x, y, z, label, `photoUrl`, `tokenType`,
  `ownerUserId`) and `remove_token`. The payload is in `payloads.rs:73`.
- **Walls and doors**: `upsert_wall`, `remove_wall`. A door is a wall with
  `doorState` `closed`/`open`, plus `locked` and `secret`
  (`payloads.rs:196-215`).
- **Lights**: `upsert_light` (radius, `brightRadius`, colour,
  `castsShadows`), and `set_ambient_light` (`dark`, `dim` or `bright`).
- **Carried light**: `set_carried_light` (tokenId, bright, dim). It is kept
  even before the token exists (`app.rs:806`).
- **Vision**: `set_token_vision` (darkvision, facing, fov, maxRange). It
  needs the token to exist first. Otherwise it is only warned about
  (`app.rs:803`).
- **Control**: `set_controlled_token`.
- **Play**: `set_scene_playing`. It defaults to **false**
  (`plugins/interaction.rs:72`). While it is false, entering a region fires
  nothing (line 299).
- **Interactives**: `upsert_interactive`. The fields are subjectKind
  `region`/`prop`/`door`, geometry, trigger `click`/`enter`, `canActivate`
  and `fireMode` `once` (`payloads.rs:265`).
- **Dice**: `trigger_dice_roll` and `set_reduced_motion`, as spec 090
  uses them.

**Direct exports** (not commands; `#[wasm_bindgen]` functions on the
module):

- `set_viewer_token(id)`: whose eyes the canvas sees through
  (`systems/lighting_vision.rs:31`).
- `set_event_callback(fn)` (`sdk.rs:356`): the one slot for events.
- Reads: `movement_state()` (`systems/token_move.rs:326`), `door_icons()`
  (`plugins/door_icons.rs:328`), `sight_probe(x, y)`
  (`plugins/darkness_probe.rs:69`), `hidden_tokens()`, `carried_lights()`,
  `list_interactives()`, `dice_landed()` and `engine_stats()`.

**Events.** Everything the engine says goes to the single
`set_event_callback` function, as a JSON string (`emit_event`, `sdk.rs:89`).
There is no polling queue for these, unlike `dice_landed()`.

- `upsert_token`, for a keyboard step or a drag
  (`systems/token_move.rs:257`, `systems/token.rs`). It carries the token's
  new x, y and `worldId`. The engine moves the token first, optimistically.
- `movement_blocked`, with `tokenId`, `wallId` and `at` (`systems/token_move.rs`).
  It is emitted for a keyboard step (lines 155, 182, 197) and for a drag
  (`systems/token.rs:437`). It uses the same rule the server uses,
  `thunderforge_canvas_core::wall::movement_blocked_by`
  (`crates/thunderforge-canvas-core/src/wall.rs:646`). That rule counts
  touching as crossing, and an open door never blocks.
- `door_icon_pressed`, with `wallId` and `icon` (`plugins/door_icons.rs:274-295`).
  The icon shows while the controlled token is within one square of the
  door. It is pressed **only by a mouse or touch click**. No key presses
  it.
- `interactionTriggered`, with `tokenId`, `interactiveId` and `trigger: "enter"`
  (`plugins/interaction.rs:328`). It fires **only when a token enters a
  region**. A click on an interactive emits nothing from the engine. In
  `apps/web` the click path goes through the app's own menu.
- `sdkError`, and the dice reads above.

**Keyboard.** WASD and the arrows move the controlled token one square
(`systems/token_move.rs:1-60`, `just_pressed`). Bevy hears keys only on the
canvas element itself. `apps/web/src/engine/canvasKeyboard.ts` works
around that by copying window key events onto the canvas. The landing's
canvas is `tabIndex=-1` and `aria-hidden`, so today no key reaches it.

**Token art.** `photoUrl` is loaded with `asset_server.load(path)`
(`app.rs:291`, `app.rs:335`), under `UnapprovedPathMode::Allow`
(`startup.rs:89-92`). The engine decodes PNG and WebP only (`Cargo.toml`,
lines 112-116). A hero's SVG `data:` URL, as Hero.tsx makes it, cannot be
token art. The demo rasterises each hero to PNG in the browser and answers
the engine's request from its network guard (`apps/demo/src/guard/rasterize.ts`,
`rest.ts:127`). The landing has no such guard.

### The demo as the server's stand-in

- `apps/demo/src/backend/` answers the app's GraphQL contract, not the
  engine. A door there is a wall plus a `click` interactive whose effect is
  `door.set_state` toggle (`handlers.ts:160-210`). `updateToken` accepts any
  move (`handlers.ts:588`). The engine's own refusal is the only wall check
  the demo has.
- The engine sandbox (`apps/engine-sandbox/src/main.ts`, line 133) drives
  the engine from plain TypeScript with `set_event_callback`. That is the
  shape the room needs.
- `apps/web/src/engine/bevy/engineEvents.ts` holds typed guards for
  `movement_blocked` (line 86), `door_icon_pressed` (line 236) and
  `interactionTriggered` (line 419). It is app code, not a package.
- **Reusable**: the sandbox's boot-and-callback shape;
  `packages/dice-throw`'s `diceCommand` (already shared by spec 090); the
  door-as-wall model; the event shapes. **Not reusable**: the demo's
  backend and guard. The room needs its own small referee.

### Telemetry disclosure (spec 086)

- `src/sections/WhatWeMeasure.tsx` says the page sends "which pages you
  view, how far you get in the demo, how long things take, errors…".
  `apps/thunderforge/src/telemetry/disclosure_tests.rs` (line 129) checks it
  word for word against Appendix A.5.
- Progress through a room on the landing is not "how far you get in the
  demo". Counting it, or counting badges, needs that wording changed in the
  appendix, the section and the test together.

## Decisions

### Made by the owner (2026-10-13)

- **The room.** One room on a real map, after the dice frame. Four goals:
  walk to the door and be refused, open the door and see light spill
  through, find the chest, and roll to open it (DC first).
- **The map.** The Proving Ground as it ships (`demo`). Credit MBRound18,
  CC BY-SA 4.0, link github.com/mbround18/vtt-maps, ShareAlike.
- **The party.** The hero section's four, for continuity.
- **A simulation, said plainly.** The page plays the server's part and says
  so on the page. It is never presented as the live server.
- **Bytes.** Zero engine bytes until the click. The engine comes from
  `/demo/` through spec 090's frame and loader. About 5MB brotli on the
  click is accepted.
- **Badges** are wanted and may be counted in telemetry, with the spec 086
  disclosure updated. That disclosure counts badges only, never progress
  through the room (owner, 2026-10-13).
- **The fallback is a reload.** If the canvas cannot move between frames,
  FR-006's reload is acceptable, history and progress lost included
  (owner, 2026-10-13).
- **Gate.** Spec 045 (walls block, doors, keyboard movement, vision) is
  built, and the playtest gate is met.
- **Accessibility is the floor.** The goals live in the DOM. A DOM-only path
  completes every goal without the canvas. Playwright proves it in the
  `landing` slice.
- **Never an AI game master.** The page is a scripted referee.

### Made by this spec

- **One hero, picked from the four.** The visitor plays one hero, chosen
  from the hero section's party, with Sir Pip as the default.
  - One controlled token means one point of view, one torch, and goals that
    mean one thing.
  - Four tokens would need token selection, which is the open keyboard
    blocker in `apps/web`. It would also make "who opened the door" a
    question.
  - The picker is four radio buttons. The pick changes the art and the
    name, nothing else.
- **The middle room, as the map draws it.** The hero starts in the dark
  middle room, four to six squares from its closed door. The chest stands in
  the lit hall beyond that door. Nothing on the map is moved or added except
  the chest and the hero. The map has exactly the room the goals need.
- **The image as is.** The engine draws `/maps/demo.webp`, the same URL the
  legend already fetched. No derivative is made. A visitor who has scrolled
  past the legend pays nothing more for the map. A cropped room is the lever
  to pull later, if the measured weight says so.
- **One engine, one canvas, moved to whoever asks.** The page holds one
  engine and one canvas. The dice tray and the room each own a frame. The
  canvas moves into the frame the visitor last asked for, and that frame's
  scene is sent again.
  - `start` is once per page, so two engines are impossible.
  - Making one section wait for the other would strand a visitor who
    already paid the download.
  - The fallback is in FR-006.
- **The referee judges every move as well as the engine.** The page plays
  the server, and the server re-judges whatever the engine shows (ADR-095).
  The referee therefore re-checks every `upsert_token` with its own copy of
  `movement_blocked_by`, touching rule included. It must not reuse Hero.tsx's
  `crossesWall`, which differs at the ends. An e2e parity check catches any
  disagreement.
- **The door opens from a DOM button as well as the icon.** The icon is
  mouse-only (`door_icons.rs:274`). The button is how a keyboard or screen
  reader opens it. Both go through the referee's one rule.
- **The chest is found by walking up to it.** The engine reports entering a
  region and nothing for a click on one. So the chest's interactive is a
  `region` with trigger `enter`, fired once, over the squares around the
  chest.
- **The roll is `1d20+2` against DC 12, for every hero.** The landing has no
  character sheets, and a per-hero bonus would be invented. The DC is shown
  before the roll. A failure may be rolled again, and each try is a new
  roll. The dice decide every try; nothing is rigged toward success.
- **Hero and chest art are built, not fetched.** A script renders each of
  the four heroes from `@thunderforge/heroes`, and a chest drawn in the
  landing's ink style, to small WebPs, with Chromium (as `make-poster.mjs`
  does). The WebPs are committed. The engine cannot read the SVGs, and the
  landing has no guard to answer for it.
- **Badges are deferred to a follow-up spec.** Badges span the hero mat,
  the dice tray and this room, so they need a page-wide event bus that this
  room should not own. Counting them changes the spec 086 appendix, the
  landing section and the Rust test together, and that should happen once,
  for all of them. This spec only leaves the hook (FR-019). It adds no
  telemetry event.
- **Placement.** The room is its own section, `#room`, straight after
  `#map`. It turns that section's claim into something the visitor does.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - A visitor plays the room on the real engine (Priority: P1)

A visitor reaches "Play the room" below the map. A sentence above it says
the page plays the server's part. They pick Sir Pip and press the button.
The poster gives way to the engine: a dark room, a torch's circle of light,
the hero in it.

They walk toward the door. The last step, into the shut door, is refused.
The note says the door is shut and that walls and closed doors refuse a
move, as the server would. They press the door's icon, or "Open the door".
The door opens, and the hall's lamplight falls through the doorway.

In the hall, at the edge of the light, is a chest. They walk up to it, and
the list says they found it. "Roll to open it: DC 12, 1d20+2" appears. They
roll. The d20 tumbles onto the floor and lands on 14, for 16 against 12.
The lid opens. Inside is a note pointing to the demo, where the rest of the
Proving Ground waits.

**Why this priority**: This is the feature the owner chose, and it is the
first thing on the page that shows the engine doing what the copy claims.

**Independent Test**: In the landing e2e, with `/demo/` served:

- press the button;
- drive the hero with keys forwarded from the room's controls;
- read the engine's own reports (`movement_state`, `door_icons`,
  `sight_probe`, `list_interactives` and `dice_landed`) at each step;
- compare them with the DOM goal list.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a first visit, **When** the page is scrolled top to bottom,
   **Then**:
   - no engine file and no room art is requested;
   - the room shows a poster, the four-hero picker, the simulation sentence
     and the map credit.
2. **Given** the visitor presses "Play the room", **When** the engine runs,
   **Then**:
   - the scene is the middle room in darkness;
   - the chosen hero carries a light, sees through `set_viewer_token`, and
     is the controlled token;
   - `set_scene_playing` is on;
   - the four goals are listed in the DOM, none done.
3. **Given** the hero beside the shut door, **When** the visitor steps into
   it, **Then**:
   - the engine emits `movement_blocked` naming the door's wall, and the
     hero does not move;
   - goal 1 is marked done;
   - the live note says once, in words, that the door refused the move.
4. **Given** the hero within one square of the door, **When** the visitor
   presses the door icon on the canvas, or the "Open the door" button,
   **Then**:
   - the referee sends the wall back with `doorState: "open"`;
   - a point in the hall that `sight_probe` reported unseen now reports
     seen;
   - goal 2 is marked done.
5. **Given** the hero is not within one square of the door, **When** the
   visitor presses "Open the door", **Then** the referee refuses it, the
   door stays shut, and the note says to step up to the door first.
6. **Given** the open door, **When** the hero enters the squares around the
   chest, **Then** the engine emits `interactionTriggered` for the chest's
   region, and goal 3 is marked done. It fires once.
7. **Given** goal 3 done, **When** the visitor rolls, **Then**:
   - the DC and the formula are on screen before the roll;
   - the roll is made with the dice crate and thrown with `diceCommand`;
   - the faces `dice_landed()` reports equal the crate's rolls;
   - the DOM states the total and whether it beat the DC no later than the
     throw lands.
   - On a success, goal 4 is done and the ending shows. On a failure, the
     visitor may roll again.
8. **Given** the room at any point, **When** the visitor presses "Start
   over", **Then** the scene, the hero, the door and the goals return to
   the start, and nothing reloads.
9. **Given** the room, **When** it renders, in any state, **Then** the
   simulation sentence and the MBRound18 / CC BY-SA 4.0 credit with its
   link are visible beside the frame.

### User Story 2 - Every goal without the canvas (Priority: P1)

A screen-reader user, a keyboard-only visitor, or a visitor whose browser
is held back plays the same room in words. "You stand in a dark room. The
door is four squares north." A pad of four move buttons, "Open the door"
and "Roll" complete every goal, and the same referee answers.

**Why this priority**: The owner made accessibility the floor. Spec 090
keeps the tray as the accessible truth; the goal list and its note are
that truth here.

**Independent Test**: Run every US1 scenario with the engine never booted,
using only the DOM controls. Then run axe and the keyboard checks with the
engine booted.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** no click on "Play the room", **When** the visitor uses the
   move pad, "Open the door" and "Roll", **Then**:
   - all four goals can be completed;
   - every move, refusal, door change, find and roll is announced once
     through one `aria-live` region;
   - no engine byte is fetched.
2. **Given** a browser held back (no WebGL2, `saveData`, `deviceMemory`
   below 4, or a maximum texture size under 4096), **When** the room
   renders, **Then** the button is replaced by the frame's reason, and the
   DOM room works as in scenario 1.
3. **Given** the engine is running and the visitor's focus is in the
   room's controls, **When** they press an arrow key or WASD, **Then** the
   key reaches the engine, and the hero steps as the engine allows. The
   page does not scroll. Keys pressed outside the room's controls, such as
   on the hero mat or in the dice formula, are never forwarded.
4. **Given** the engine is running, **When** axe runs, **Then** it reports
   zero violations, at 390, 768 and 1440 px, in light and dark. The canvas
   is `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order.
5. **Given** keyboard only, **When** the visitor tabs from the picker to
   the button, the pad, the door button, the roll and "Start over", **Then**
   every stop has a visible focus indicator and is not covered by the
   frame.
6. **Given** reduced motion, **When** the room plays, **Then**:
   - the engine gets `set_reduced_motion` before anything moves;
   - the camera moves with `immediate`;
   - the dice take the contract's reduced time;
   - nothing in the DOM animates beyond the page's reduced-motion rules.

### User Story 3 - The dice tray and the room share one table (Priority: P1)

A visitor throws dice in the tray, then scrolls to the room and presses
"Play the room". The room starts at once, with no second download. The
tray's frame goes back to its poster, with "The table is in the room below.
Bring it back." Pressing that moves the table back up, and the tray throws
again.

**Why this priority**: Without it, the second frame a visitor presses
reports "running" and draws nothing (`startup.rs:43`). That is a broken
front page.

**Independent Test**: In the landing e2e, boot the tray's frame, throw,
then boot the room. Check that:

- no engine file is fetched twice;
- the canvas element is the same one;
- the room's scene is drawn;
- throwing in the tray after moving the table back is reported by
  `dice_landed()`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the engine running in the tray, **When** the visitor presses
   "Play the room", **Then**:
   - the one canvas moves into the room's frame and the room's scene is
     sent;
   - the tray's frame shows its poster and a "Bring it back" button;
   - no engine file is requested again.
2. **Given** the room has the table, **When** the visitor rolls in the
   tray, **Then** the tray prints the result as before. No throw is sent
   while the table is elsewhere.
3. **Given** the room started first, **When** the visitor presses "Throw it
   on the table" in the tray, **Then** the table moves to the tray, the
   room's scene is cleared from it, and the room shows its poster. The
   room's DOM goals keep their state.
4. **Given** either frame holds the table, **When** it leaves the viewport,
   **Then** drawing pauses as spec 090 requires.

### Edge Cases

- **Keys before the hero exists.** Keys forwarded before setup has placed
  the hero move nothing and announce nothing. `movement_state()` explains
  why.
- **Engine and referee disagree.** The engine allows a step the referee
  refuses, or the reverse. The referee's answer stands. The token is sent
  back to its last accepted square. The e2e parity check (FR-012) fails.
- **A drag.** The hero is dragged with a mouse across a wall. The engine
  refuses it (`token.rs:437`), and the referee re-judges the drop like any
  other move. The goals count a drag the same as a step.
- **Pointer under scale.** The stage is CSS-scaled (spec 090). A click on
  the door icon must land on the icon at every width. If the scaled stage
  maps pointers wrongly, the frame is fixed in this spec. The DOM button
  still works either way.
- **Small screens.** At 390px the frame, the pad and the goals stack. The
  pad stays reachable without scrolling past the canvas.
- **Map texture limits.** A device whose `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE` is under 4096
  cannot draw the map. It is held back (US2 scenario 2) and is not shown a
  broken board.
- **The legend not yet seen.** A visitor who opens the room before the
  legend has rendered fetches `/maps/demo.webp` (1.95MB) on the click. The
  readout counts the engine only; the map's weight is stated beside the
  button.
- **Tab hidden mid-throw.** The roll's DOM result still prints. A skipped
  throw is not an error (spec 090).
- **Rapid presses.** Pressing "Open the door" twice, or rolling twice
  before the throw lands, changes state once per accepted action. Each
  announcement is made once.
- **The engine stops** (a panic). The frame fails as spec 090 says. The DOM
  room keeps its state and goes on in words.
- **Telemetry off.** Nothing new is sent in any case. The frame's existing
  failure report follows `TELEMETRY=false` as today.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

**The table, shared (US3)**

- **FR-001**: The landing MUST hold at most one engine and one canvas per
  page, in one module (the *engine host*). The host loads the engine once,
  through spec 090's `loadEngine`, and creates the canvas itself, outside
  React's tree, so moving it never fights React.
- **FR-002**: `EngineFrame` MUST take the table from the host instead of
  starting its own. The frame the visitor last pressed holds the canvas. A
  frame that loses it MUST return to its poster with a button to bring it
  back. Bringing it back MUST NOT fetch any engine file again.
- **FR-003**: When a frame takes the table, its scene MUST be sent again
  from scratch (`setup`). The scene it replaces MUST be removed first:
  every token, wall, light, interactive and background the previous scene
  sent. The tray therefore gets an explicit scene of its own (ambient light,
  no viewer token) instead of relying on the engine's start state, which the
  room changes.
- **FR-004**: The host MUST own the single `set_event_callback` and hand
  each event to the frame that holds the table.
- **FR-005**: `loadEngine`'s `Engine` type MUST name every export this spec
  uses: `set_event_callback`, `set_viewer_token`, and the reads in FR-021.
- **FR-006**: The plan's first task MUST prove in Chromium that a booted
  canvas can move between frames and keep drawing, its WebGL context and
  its keyboard input. If it cannot, the fallback is:
  - the second frame says where the table is and offers "Move the table
    here";
  - that reloads the page with the target in the URL, and the engine boots
    there from the browser cache.
  The fallback MUST be recorded in the plan with the evidence. The owner
  accepted the reload on 2026-10-13.

**The room (US1)**

- **FR-007**: The room MUST be its own lazy section, `#room`, after `#map`,
  with a heading, the simulation sentence, the hero picker, the frame, the
  goal list, the controls and the map credit.
- **FR-008**: The simulation sentence MUST say, on the page and above the
  frame, that this room runs in the visitor's browser and the page plays
  the server's part. The room's copy MUST NOT call it live, connected,
  online or multiplayer.
- **FR-009**: The room's scene MUST be built from `/maps/demo.json` and
  `/maps/demo.webp` as the landing already serves them:
  - every wall and door, each given a stable id by the referee;
  - every light;
  - the grid at 117;
  - ambient light `dark`;
  - the camera fitted to the middle room and the hall.
  No other wall, light or token may be added except the hero and the
  chest.
- **FR-010**: The hero MUST be one of the four `PARTY` presets, picked in
  the DOM (Sir Pip by default). Setup MUST happen in this order:
  1. `upsert_token` with built WebP art and `tokenType: "character"`;
  2. `set_token_vision`, `set_carried_light`, `set_controlled_token` and
     `set_viewer_token`;
  3. `set_scene_playing: true`.
  Changing the pick MUST start the room over with the new hero.
- **FR-011**: The referee MUST be a scripted state machine in the page,
  with no network and no randomness except the dice crate's. It MUST:
  - accept or refuse every `upsert_token` the engine emits for the hero;
  - send the last accepted position back only when it overturns the engine
    (a refusal); an accepted move is never echoed, so a route never
    stutters;
  - set door state on a door request;
  - judge the roll against the DC;
  - mark goals.
  It MUST NOT generate text, choose outcomes, or call any model. It is a
  referee, not a game master.
- **FR-012**: The referee's movement rule MUST match
  `thunderforge_canvas_core::wall::movement_blocked_by`. That means
  touching counts, an open door never blocks, and a move to the same point
  is never blocked. The e2e MUST prove parity: for every step the e2e takes
  in the room, including steps into each wall of the middle room and into
  the shut door, the referee's verdict equals the engine's
  (`movement_blocked` emitted or not). Because a refusal rolls the hero
  back, the page cannot show a disagreement after the fact. So:
  - the room logs any disagreement with `console.error`, which every
    landing e2e fails on through the console guard;
  - the parity e2e works out the rule's verdict in Node from the package.
  The Rust rule compares in f32, and centred half-square coordinates can
  pass 2^24, where f32 and f64 disagree. The port rounds with
  `Math.fround`, and a unit test pins a case where they differ.
- **FR-013**: The goals MUST be exactly these, in order. Each MUST be
  marked from what the referee accepted, never from the canvas alone.
  1. **Walk to the door**: done when a step into the shut door is refused.
  The map has two doors. Only the middle room's door to the hall
  (`door-hall`) counts for goals 1 and 2; the door button acts on whichever
  door is in reach and names it.
  2. **Open the door**: done when the referee opens it. That needs the hero
     within one square of the door, the same rule as the icon
     (`thunderforge_canvas_core::door_icon`).
  3. **Find the chest**: done on the chest region's
     `interactionTriggered`, or the DOM path's equivalent step.
  4. **Open the chest**: done on a roll of `1d20+2` that meets DC 12.
- **FR-014**: The chest MUST be an object token with built art, standing in
  the hall within reach of the hall's lamp. It MUST have one `region`
  interactive with trigger `enter` and `fireMode: "once"` over the squares
  around it.
- **FR-015**: The roll MUST use `@thunderforge/dice` (the chunk the tray
  already loads) and `packages/dice-throw`'s `diceCommand`. The DC and
  formula MUST be shown before the roll. The DOM result is the truth, as in
  spec 090.
- **FR-016**: The ending MUST say what the visitor just used ("the engine
  your table runs on"). It MUST link to the demo and repeat the map credit.
  It MUST NOT gate anything.
- **FR-017**: "Start over" MUST restore the starting scene and goals without
  reloading or refetching.
- **FR-018**: The hero and chest WebPs MUST be produced by a script with
  Chromium, from `@thunderforge/heroes` and an SVG kept in the landing, and
  committed. The poster MUST be made the same way, from the engine in the
  room's starting state, as `make-poster.mjs` makes the dice poster.
- **FR-019**: Each goal reached MUST be dispatched on `window` as one
  `thunderforge:encounter-goal` event, with the goal's id from a closed set
  of four. `telemetry.ts` MUST NOT listen to it in this spec. It is the hook
  the badges spec attaches to.

**Without the canvas (US2)**

- **FR-020**: The DOM room MUST offer, at all times and without the
  engine:
  - a four-button move pad;
  - "Open the door";
  - "Roll" (once goal 3 is done);
  - "Start over";
  - a text description of where the hero stands and what is near.
  The same referee answers the DOM room and the canvas, and one `aria-live`
  region announces each outcome once.
- **FR-021**: While the engine runs, the move pad and the arrow and WASD
  keys pressed inside the room's controls MUST be forwarded to the canvas
  as key events, so the engine's own rule refuses first. Forwarding MUST be
  scoped to the room's controls, never the window. The canvas MUST stay
  `aria-hidden` and `tabIndex=-1`. Spec 090's stage has
  `pointer-events: none` so the page keeps the mouse wheel; the room's
  frame takes the pointer (for the door icon), and the wheel is stopped
  before the engine sees it, so the page still scrolls.
- **FR-022**: The room's capability check MUST add, to spec 090's checks, a
  WebGL2 `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE` of at least 4096. A browser below it reports a
  new `engine.load_failed` reason, `small_texture`. Spec 086's
  `contracts/browser-events.md` lists those reasons as a closed set, so it
  gains the row in the same commit. This is the room's only telemetry
  change.
- **FR-023**: Every check spec 090 runs on the page MUST pass with the room
  rendered, booted and completed: axe, focus, overlap, overflow, clipping,
  console, and per-section screenshots at three widths and two schemes.

**Proof**

- **FR-024**: The landing e2e MUST cover US1 through the engine and US2
  without it. It reads the engine through the page's own module, as
  `engine-frame.spec.ts` does, using:
  - `movement_state()` for the hero's square;
  - `door_icons()` for the icon shown;
  - `sight_probe(x, y)` for light through the door;
  - `list_interactives()` for the chest;
  - `dice_landed()` for the throw.
- **FR-025**: The e2e MUST record every request and fail if any engine
  file, room art or `/maps/demo.webp` beyond the legend's own request is
  fetched before the click.

### Key Entities

- **Engine host**: the page's one engine, its one canvas, its one event
  callback, and which frame holds them.
- **Room script**: the room's fixed facts. These are the map file, the
  wall ids it assigns, the hero's start square, the chest's square and
  region, the hall point used for the light check, the formula and the DC.
- **Referee**: the state machine that plays the server. It holds the
  hero's accepted square, the door's state, the goals and the roll history.
- **Goal**: one of four ids (`door-reached`, `door-opened`, `chest-found`,
  `chest-opened`), with done/not done and the sentence announced when it
  is done.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A first visit, scrolled top to bottom, transfers **0 bytes**
  of engine files and room art, checked by request log.
- **SC-002**: A visitor who has thrown dice and then plays the room
  downloads **no engine bytes** the second time. The canvas element is the
  same one.
- **SC-003**: All four goals complete in the e2e twice: once through the
  engine, once DOM-only. The DOM-only run makes **no** engine request.
- **SC-004**: The referee and the engine agree on **every** step in the
  parity run (FR-012), with **zero** disagreements.
- **SC-005**: Axe finds zero violations on the page with the room at rest,
  booted and completed, at 390, 768 and 1440 px, in light and dark.
- **SC-006**: Every throw's faces in `dice_landed()` equal the crate's
  rolls for the same seed.
- **SC-007**: A scripted e2e run from the click to the chest opening, on a
  fixed seed that succeeds, finishes in under 60 seconds of page time, with
  reduced motion.
- **SC-008**: The landing slice passes three times in a row.

### Proof

The changes are proven with the `landing` slice, plus:

- `pnpm -F @thunderforge/demo e2e`, only if the demo's build or manifest
  changes;
- `make lint` and `pnpm verify`.

The full e2e suite is not a gate.

## Assumptions

- The engine runs the room without `set_world`, as the sandbox and spec 090
  do. Its network stays idle, and any stray request would fail the console
  check (spec 090 FR-007).
- The engine's world coordinates for the map match `demo.json`'s, centred
  on the map, as the legend already draws them. The plan confirms the axis
  direction and fixes "north" for the DOM descriptions from it.
- `set_viewer_token` with ambient `dark` and the hero's carried light makes
  the room dark outside the torch and the hall lit by its own lights, as
  spec 045 built. A closed door blocks vision, so the hall point is unseen
  until the door opens.
- No new dependency is needed. Chromium (Playwright) already renders the
  dice poster.
- The `landing` slice's paths already cover `apps/landing/**`. If the art
  script reads `packages/heroes`, that path is added to the slice.

## Out of scope

- **Achievement badges** and any telemetry for the room or for badges. They
  are the follow-up spec, which also changes the spec 086 Appendix A.5,
  `WhatWeMeasure.tsx` and `disclosure_tests.rs` together.
- More than one hero on the board, monsters, combat, turn order or
  initiative.
- Other maps, a cropped room image, or editing The Proving Ground.
- Exploration memory (`set_exploration`).
- Changing Hero.tsx's wall rule to match the engine's.
- Any change to `apps/web` or the demo, beyond the demo's build if FR-006's
  fallback needs it.
