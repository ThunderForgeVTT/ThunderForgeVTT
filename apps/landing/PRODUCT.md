# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite, a pnpm workspace app at `apps/landing`, matching the
repository's other web apps. Static output, served from the cluster at
`thunderforge.dev`, with the browser demo (`apps/demo`, built for `/demo/`)
bundled into the same image.

## Users

Tabletop players and game masters (D&D 5e first, other systems after) who are
tired of their current virtual tabletop: people who have lost data to major
version upgrades, waited on stalled paid modules, or watched a rules platform
push a new edition over the material they loved. They arrive curious, from a
link, a post, or GitHub, and decide in a minute whether this is worth
following, funding, or self-hosting.

## Product Purpose

ThunderForgeVTT is an open source (AGPL-3.0-or-later), self-hosted virtual
tabletop built by one person as a passion project. It exists to help the
fantasy happen and stay out of its way: in person, online, or both at once,
easy to host, easy to use, resilient. The homepage succeeds when a visitor
follows the project on GitHub, donates (GitHub Sponsors or Ko-fi), tries the
browser demo, or stands up their own instance.

## Positioning

Free to self-host forever, from source, with one `docker compose up`. A Rust
server is the single authority; every client renders what the server
approved. The board is a Bevy engine compiled to WebAssembly. Game systems
are packs, not forks. The creator's hard stance: AI may assist a human game
master, never replace one; the community at the table is the point.

## Operating Context

Visitors read the page on desktop and phone. The browser demo at `/demo/`
runs a real world locally in the page, no account and no server. Development
happens in the open on GitHub (repository, Discussions, Projects). No public
hosted instance exists yet; the current objective is a 5e group playing end to
end: sign up, build a table, put characters on a map, roll, fight, come back
next week.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shipped and demonstrable: walls, doors, lights, sight, snapping, structures,
  shapes, rolls at the table, combat in the browser, touchscreen play,
  resumable downloads, character claiming, per-system packs.
- Game system packs in the repo: 5E System Core (SRD 5.2.1), Pathfinder Second
  Edition, Blades in the Dark, Fate Core, Cypher System, Year Zero Engine,
  Roll for Shoes, Genie (house system), Basic Game System. 5e and Roll for
  Shoes are the field-test targets; the rest must keep working.
- Not yet released; no release date. Do not promise one.
- Never call a user-authored content set a "pack" or "bundle"; it is a
  "collection".

## Product Principles

1. The fantasy first; the tool gets out of the way.
2. Yours to keep: self-hosted, open source, no edition or subscription can
   take a table away.
3. One authority: the server decides, every screen agrees.
4. A human runs the game.
5. Honest numbers or none.
