Mode: Persuade. The visitor follows on GitHub, donates, tries /demo/, or self-hosts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the table. A wet-erase grid mat where the story is drawn in marker and the reader can push real tokens around. It refuses the category default: dark map art, glowing CTA, feature card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool vinyl white (#eceee9) printed with a pale blue-grey one-inch grid, every fifth line darker. Four translucent wet-erase inks, black #1f2a2e, blue #2a62c9, red #c4352a, green #2f9a54, round caps, overshot corners. Permanent Marker for anything hand-drawn, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for formulas and measurements. Controls are inked squares and outlines on the mat, never stock pills.

STORY: A passion project by one person who got burned by upgrades, editions and stalled modules. It is free and self-hosted, in person, online or both. The proof is that the real dice and real tokens run right here. The asks are to play the demo, donate and star.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed mat. A black-marker room snapped to the grid holds the headline "Get back to the fantasy." at display scale, a one-line offer, and three inked actions: Play the demo world (primary, blue fill), Donate (red), Star on GitHub. A door in the east wall opens onto a party of hero tokens and a goblin pack. The tokens are draggable and snap to squares. A green marker note invites the drag.

FORM: The Battle Mat. Position 1 on my ordered list (the pick). Seed key c7472f53. Signature interaction: grid-snapping token drag. Motion: the room's walls draw on once at load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
