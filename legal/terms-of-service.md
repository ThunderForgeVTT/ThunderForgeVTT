## What you make here

**Your content stays yours.** Worlds, characters, items, lore, maps — you keep
whatever rights you had in them. Nothing here takes ownership of your work.

You give the operator permission to store and display your content so the
service can function: showing your world to the players you invited, keeping
revision history, and making backups. That permission exists to run the service
and for nothing else. It ends when you delete the content, except where a backup
has not yet rotated out or where a legal record must be kept.

**You are responsible for what you upload.** Specifically: that you have the
right to use it. Material from a published game supplement, a map you found
online, artwork someone else drew — putting these into a world is your decision
and your liability. This matters more than it might seem, because tabletop
material is heavily copyrighted and copying it is easy.

If someone reports your content as infringing, it is handled through the
[DMCA policy](/legal/dmca). You will be told, and you can dispute it.

## What the software ships with

Official game-system packs are distributed under their own open licences — the
ORC Licence, Creative Commons, the Cypher System Open Licence, Free League's
Foundry Licence, and others. Each pack states its licence and required
attribution in the application. Those licences govern that content, not these
terms, and following them is a condition of using it.
