## Running this instance makes you responsible for it

You are the operator of this instance. That means the legal obligations of
everything inside it are yours: the content people upload to it, the notices
filed against it, and the law wherever it runs.

This software does not host your instance, does not see what is on it, and
cannot remove anything from it. Nobody else can answer for it on your behalf.

## Copyright notices come to you

People will publish things from this instance — collections, characters, items
and abilities, readable by anyone holding a link. Some of what they publish will
not be theirs to publish.

You need an address a copyright notice can reach, and this instance will not
publish anything until it has one. Where your jurisdiction lets a service limit
its liability by registering a designated agent, **registering one is your own
act**. This software does not do it for you, has not done it, and cannot.

## The people on your instance are trusting you with their work

Their characters, their maps, their campaigns and their notes are on a machine
you control. Back it up, keep it patched, and be reachable when something goes
wrong.

If you stop running it, tell them before you stop.
